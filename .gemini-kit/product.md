# Product Context

**Product:**
jordanhindo.dev is a dual-purpose platform: it is a personal engineering portfolio showcasing "Agentic AI" projects (Asset Hatch, Catwalk, Turtlez) and a live content hub for technical writing on AI orchestration. It serves as the primary artifact for Jordan Hindo's job search, proving his capability as a "Founding Agentic Engineer."

**Users:**
1. **Recruiters & CTOs:** Visiting to verify technical claims (Founders of Wayline, FurtherAI, Scaled Cognition).
2. **AI Engineers:** Reading the blog series (Asset Hatch 1-18) to learn about multi-agent orchestration.
3. **Jordan Hindo:** Using it as a "Resume API" and tracking job applications via the `marketing/` content collection.

**Goals:**
1. **Convert Visits to Interviews:** Demonstrate "Senior Independent Engineer" status (3+ years effective experience) through deep technical content.
2. **Prove the Methodology:** The site itself is built using the "AI-Native Loop" (Claude Vision -> Antigravity Build -> Perplexity Research -> Verification), proving the workflow works.
3. **Host Live Demos:** Provide direct links and credentials to deployed apps (Asset Hatch).

**Main Features:**
- **Blog Series Engine:** Renders complex markdown with "Part X of Y" navigation (Asset Hatch series).
- **Project Showcase:** Detailed case studies for Hatch-Studios, Catwalk, and TheFeed with technical tags (MCP, RAG, Geospatial).
- **Resume Page:** A live, print-friendly CV optimized for "Agentic Engineer" roles.
- **Job Tracker (Internal):** A hidden markdown-based CRM for tracking applications and follow-ups.
