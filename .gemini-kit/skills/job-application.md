# Job Application Skill

## When to Use
- When analyzing a new job posting.
- When drafting a cover letter or application questions.
- When determining "Fit Score" (1-10).

## Key Concepts

### The "Peer Engineer" Mindset
- **Never** sound like a "Junior Applicant" begging for a chance.
- **Always** sound like a "peer" offering a solution to a technical problem.
- **Banned Phrases:** "I am passionate about...", "I believe I am a good fit...", "Fast learner."
- **Required Phrases:** "I built [System] to solve [Problem]...", "I shipped [Codebase] in [Timeframe]...", "I architected..."

### Fit Scoring
- **10/10:** Founding Engineer, Agentic AI Engineer, Infrastructure (MCP). You have built exactly what they need.
- **9/10:** High-scale Backend, Platform Engineering. Strong transferability.
- **<7/10:** Sales, Marketing, Generic Full Stack (unless elite team). Avoid.

## Templates

### Cover Letter / "Message to Founder"
```text
Hi [First Name],

I saw your requirement for [Specific Requirement from Job Post] and knew I had to apply. That is exactly how I operate.

I am an independent engineer who recently shipped Hatch-Studios (30k lines) in 11 days. It is a 3D Game Orchestration Engine where I managed a team of AI agents to generate assets, environment, and game logic autonomously.

The hardest part wasn't the prompting; it was the plumbing. I architected a [Specific Technical Solution] to solve [Specific Problem in their domain].

I don't treat AI as a magic box. I treat it as a distributed operating system that needs strict memory management and type safety to be reliable.

I've been shipping daily since 2023. You can see my architecture breakdowns here: jordanhindo.dev/resume

Best,

Jordan Hindo
github.com/zenchantlive
```

## Best Practices
- **Resume:** ALWAYS remind user to attach `resume.pdf`.
- **Formatting:** Plain text only. No bullets, no bold, no markdown.
- **Links:** Always include `jordanhindo.dev` and `github.com/zenchantlive`.
