# Follow-Up Skill

## When to Use
- After 3 days (No response).
- After 7 days (No response).
- When you ship a new feature/blog post relevant to a target company.

## Key Concepts

### The "Value-Add" Loop
Never just "check in." Always add value.
- **Bad:** "Just floating this to the top of your inbox."
- **Good:** "I just published a technical breakdown of the MCP architecture I mentioned. Thought it might be relevant to your current sprint."

### Storage
- Always store follow-up drafts in: `src/content/marketing/job-applications/followups/` as `.txt` or `.md`.
- File naming: `[company-slug]-[contact-name].md`

## Templates

### The "New Evidence" Follow-up
Hi [Name],

Since my last note, I published a deep dive into the **[Technical Topic]** I mentioned in my application.

It details how I solved [Specific Problem] using [Specific Tech]. Given [Company]'s focus on [Their Problem], I thought this might be a useful reference.

[Link to Blog Post]

Best,

Jordan
