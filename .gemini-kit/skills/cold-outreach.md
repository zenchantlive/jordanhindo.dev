# Cold Outreach Skill

## When to Use
- When sending a DM to a Founder/CTO (Email or LinkedIn).
- When bypassing the ATS "Black Hole."

## Key Concepts

### The Hook
- You must prove you are "Signal" in 3 seconds.
- **Bad Hook:** "I applied for the role and wanted to follow up."
- **Good Hook:** "I built the exact [MCP/Agentic] infrastructure you are hiring for."

### Channel Strategy
1. **Email:** Priority 1. Pattern: `first@company.com` or `first.last@company.com`.
2. **LinkedIn Connection Note:** Priority 2. 300 chars max.
3. **Twitter DM:** Priority 3. Only if they have open DMs.

## Examples

### Email (Infrastructure Focus)
**Subject:** Agentic AI Engineer candidate: [Tech Topic] - Jordan Hindo

Hi [First Name],

I applied for the [Role Name]. I noticed you are solving [Problem, e.g., Reliability/Latency].

I recently architected [Project] to solve exactly that. I built a [Technical Solution, e.g., Hybrid Persistence Layer] that prevents context drift in long-running agent loops.

I shipped this in 11 days solo. I'd love to show you the architecture.

Best,

Jordan Hindo
jordanhindo.dev

### LinkedIn Note (300 chars)
Hi [Name], I applied for the [Role]. I specialize in rapid agentic prototyping—just shipped a 30k-line system (Asset Hatch) in 11 days. I noticed you’re building [Project]. I’d love to show you my workflow for automating the security layer. Best, Jordan jordanhindo.dev
